№08Questions

Asked and
answered.

01

Join the early access list
01Is pairwise a bank?

No. pairwise is a design concept. It opens no accounts and holds no money.

02Where would the interest come from?

In a real product, balances would sit with partner banks and money-market funds. The rates on this page are illustrative.

03What is the card made of?

Plus is brushed titanium and Reserve is gold-plated steel. Standard is recycled plastic in an ivory finish.

04How does the rate work?

You see the mid-market rate and a flat 0.35% fee before you confirm. The rate holds for 30 seconds.

05How does freezing work?

One tap turns the card off for every purchase. Tap again and it is back on.

06Can I send to other banks?

In the concept, transfers to other Philippine and US banks take one business day. Between pairwise accounts they are instant.

07Can I sign up?

Not for real. The early access form below is part of the concept and sends nothing.
